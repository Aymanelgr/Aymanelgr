# Aymanelgr

Building practical AI applications, from document processing to self-hosted business tools.

## Projects

### Veritas AI
Local document intelligence for purchase orders, invoices, and contracts.
Combines OCR, structured extraction, document matching, evidence-based verification,
and retrieval-augmented Q&A with human review.

**Tools:** Python, FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL/pgvector, Ollama, Redis/RQ, and n8n.

### Tawjeeh CRM
A self-hosted education CRM for campaign forms, lead scoring, advisor assignment,
recommendations, and sales workflows, with role-based access through Keycloak.

**Tools:** Python, FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, Redis/RQ, Keycloak, Nginx, and Docker.

Both projects are in active development. Source repositories are currently private.
